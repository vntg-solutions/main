# Vantage Solutions – Main Website Content Structure

This document outlines the content and section layout for **vntg.solutions**, so you can hand it directly to a designer or implement it consistently. The site has two main pillars: **client services** and **in-house products**, with special emphasis on the newly launched **CareCue** app.

---

## 1. Homepage flow (top to bottom)

### 1.1 Hero
- **Purpose:** First impression; state clearly that Vantage does both **client work** and **own products**.
- **Content:**
  - Headline that reflects both: e.g. “We build for clients and for ourselves.”
  - Subhead: client services (websites, portals, tools, automation) + in-house products (e.g. CareCue).
  - Primary CTA: “Get started” or “Book intro” (for client work).
  - Secondary CTA: “See CareCue” or “Our products” (for product showcase).
- **Design note:** Keep hero concise; avoid crowding. Consider a short rotating line or badge (e.g. “Client services” / “CareCue”) to reinforce the dual focus.

### 1.2 Product showcase – CareCue (new)
- **Purpose:** Highlight the new CareCue app; drive awareness and downloads.
- **Content:**
  - Section label: e.g. “Our products” or “From our studio”.
  - CareCue block:
    - App name + tagline: “CareCue – Your personal health companion. Track water, sleep, medications & more.”
    - Short value line: “Care that fits into your day, not the other way around.”
    - Four pillars (from carecue.vntg.solutions):
      - Water intake tracking
      - Sleep companion (tracking, insights)
      - Medication reminders
      - Appointments & routines
    - Trust line: “Private by default • Data stays on your device.”
    - CTAs: “Download on Google Play” + “Learn more” (to carecue.vntg.solutions).
  - **Assets:** Use CareCue logo (heart + checkmark), feature/banner image, and 1–2 app screenshots (e.g. Water, Sleep) for visual impact.
- **Design note:** Visually distinct from “client services” (e.g. card, gradient, or alternate background) so the product feels like a hero moment.

### 1.3 Client services
- **Purpose:** Explain what Vantage delivers for clients (e.g. Devi and others).
- **Content:**
  - Section label: “Services” or “What we build for you”.
  - Headline: e.g. “Websites, tools, and automation for small business.”
  - Service cards (existing bento):
    - Website creation
    - Bill & document generation
    - Automation & tools
    - Client portals
  - Optional: short line like “Practical solutions you can use right away.”

### 1.4 How it works / Client portals
- **Purpose:** Show how client engagements work; use Devi as a concrete example.
- **Content:**
  - Headline: e.g. “Your business online – custom sites and client-facing portals.”
  - Short explanation: custom sites and portals (e.g. devi.vntg.solutions), branded and clear.
  - Differentiators: “Ready in days, not months”, “Simple and secure”.
  - Visual: mockup or screenshot of devi.vntg.solutions (or similar portal).
  - CTA: “See client portal example” (link to devi.vntg.solutions).

### 1.5 Trust / Who we work with
- **Purpose:** Build credibility; keep it general (e.g. small businesses, teams).
- **Content:**
  - Label: “Who we work with”.
  - Short line: e.g. “Small businesses and teams that want to run smarter.”
  - Optional: logo strip, case names, or “Partner with us” CTA.

### 1.6 Contact
- **Purpose:** Convert visitors (clients and product users) into leads or conversations.
- **Content:**
  - Headline: e.g. “Ready to simplify?”
  - Short line: “Tell us what you need – a website, bill generator, automation, or something else.”
  - Form or “Book intro” CTA.
  - Footer: © Vantage Solutions, vntg.solutions; optional links to CareCue site and client portal example.

---

## 2. Global navigation

- **Services** – anchor to “Client services” section.
- **Products** or **CareCue** – anchor to “Product showcase” or dedicated CareCue block.
- **How it works** – anchor to “How it works / Client portals”.
- **Contact** – anchor to contact section.
- Optional header links:
  - “Client portal” (e.g. devi.vntg.solutions).
  - “CareCue” (carecue.vntg.solutions) or “Our app”.

---

## 3. Key messages to keep consistent

| Audience        | Message |
|----------------|---------|
| Potential clients | We build websites, portals, bill tools, and automation for small business; see Devi as an example. |
| Product users  | CareCue is our health companion app: water, sleep, meds, appointments; private and designed for real-life routines. |
| Both           | Vantage Solutions: client work + in-house products under vntg.solutions. |

---

## 4. CareCue assets (for main site)

- **Logo:** CareCue app icon (blue heart + checkmark in rounded square).
- **Feature/banner:** Promo image (e.g. blue background, phone mockup, feature list).
- **Screenshots (optional):** Welcome screen, Water Intake, Sleep Tracker – for “Product showcase” or a small gallery.

Place logo and feature image in `public/assets/` (e.g. `carecue-logo.png`, `carecue-feature.png`) for use in the Product showcase section.

You can hand this structure to your designer for wireframes and visual design, and to your copywriter for final headlines and microcopy. The order (Hero → Products → Services → How it works → Trust → Contact) makes the dual focus clear and gives CareCue prominent placement without overshadowing client services.
